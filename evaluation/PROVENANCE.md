# Evaluation asset provenance

The ParcelFlow fixture, three task definitions, hidden tests, and initial answer/score templates were migrated from the earlier `dsh-comprehension-headless/evaluation/` experiment on August 20, 2026.

Both projects are maintained by The Agentic Survivor project and distributed under the MIT License. The migrated assets are now maintained in this repository. Running this evaluation does not require the earlier repository, DeepSeek Harness, its plugin, or a DSH profile.

The Ruby study runner in this repository was rewritten for Pi-imported runs, three review conditions, and a single-reviewer formative study. It is not a copy of the former DSH task runner.
